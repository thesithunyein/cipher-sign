CipherSign is a locked company wallet for payouts. You choose who can get paid and the maximum amount. If a payment breaks those rules, it is refused. Built on Flare so the key cannot quietly change the rules.

Watch 2‑min demo

Real problem

Ops bots and fee scripts can drain funds if a hot key is stolen or a bug fires a huge transfer.

Real users

Finance ops, automation teams, and reward managers who need payouts that stay inside a budget.

Real control

Approved people only. Spending limit. Optional end date. Break the rules and the vault says no.

How it works

  1. Set rules: who can get paid and the spending limit
  2. Lock them: rules sit in secure hardware on Flare
  3. Send payouts: approved ones go through; bad ones are blocked
