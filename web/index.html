Who it is for

  • Finance and ops leads who approve payouts
  • Teams that pay contractors, fees, or rewards with scripts
  • Anyone who needs a hard stop on overspend

Who it is not for

  • Trading or swapping tokens
  • Buying crypto as a consumer
  • People looking for a wallet app

Control

Only people you approve can get paid.

Budget

A hard spending limit the vault cannot ignore.

Safety

Bad payouts are refused before money moves.

How it works

  1. Set rules: who can get paid and the spending limit
  2. Lock them: rules are secured so they cannot quietly change
  3. Send payouts: good ones go through, bad ones are blocked
