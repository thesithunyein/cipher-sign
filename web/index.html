Q&A

Optional — tap a question if you need it.

Who is this for?

Teams that send fees, payroll, or rewards and need to stop overpay / wrong-person mistakes.

What does Connect do?

Connects your wallet on Flare Coston2 and checks the CipherSign TEE. Lock and Approve submit real InstructionSender transactions on-chain.

Where do I get wallet addresses?

From MetaMask (or any wallet): copy the account address, paste into Rules.

What gets blocked?

Anything over your limit, or paid to someone not on your approved list.

Is this a trading app?

No — payout control only. Not for swapping or trading tokens.

How do I know an approval is real?

Approve sends an on-chain InstructionSender.sign tx on Coston2 (explorer link). The TEE then returns a signature; the app recovers the vault signer and matches it. No explorer tx = not a real product approval.
